Proven track record in orchestrating multi-node distributed Kubernetes (K3s) clusters over mesh networks, architecting in-cluster autonomous CI/CD pipelines, engineering intelligent document extraction systems (OCR & vision LLMs), and delivering enterprise-grade middleware connecting Tier-1 ERPs (Oracle NetSuite, Odoo), collaborative workspaces (Lark Base), and financial engines across 50+ production microservices.

---

### 🏛️ Key Enterprise Case Studies & Accomplishments

#### 📄 1. Intelligent Procurement & Purchase Order (PO) Extraction Pipeline
- **Problem & Architecture**: Manual entry of multi-page, non-standardized supplier quotes and complex Purchase Orders (POs) was prone to human error and created operational bottlenecks.
- **Solution**: Engineered an automated document intelligence pipeline combining high-precision computer vision, OCR preprocessing, and multimodal LLMs.
- **Outcome**: Converts unstructured PDFs and image scans into normalized, validated tabular data with line-item tax and SKU reconciliation. Seamlessly streams data directly into Lark Base tables and enterprise procurement ERPs, reducing manual data entry time by over 85%.

#### 🏭 2. Manufacturing Execution System (MES) & Shopfloor Tracking
- **Production Line Control**: Architected a custom shopfloor MES for a leading commercial seafood processing enterprise. Integrated barcode weighing stations, cold-chain batch tracking, and real-time yield monitoring directly with an on-premise **Odoo ERP** backend.
- **Industrial Formulation ERP**: Designed manufacturing execution modules for high-precision aluminium tube fabrication, managing multi-stage extrusion scheduling, raw alloy formulation, quality inspection gates, and inventory lot traceability.

#### 🏢 3. Enterprise Smart Building Management System (BMS)
- **High-Rise Commercial Facility Platform**: Designed and built an end-to-end 8-module BMS platform utilizing **Next.js 16 + PostgreSQL 18**.
- **Capabilities**: Encompasses automated tenant tenancy lifecycles, tiered utility billing calculation, preventive maintenance work orders, IoT sensor anomaly alarms, visitor gate clearance, and facility asset depreciation tracking.

#### 📦 4. Warehouse & Asset Logistics (WMS / AMS)
- **High-Value Equipment Asset Tracking**: Developed an integrated WMS and Asset Management System for a tier-1 distributor of premium commercial fitness equipment (Technogym partner).
- **Features**: Serialized asset lifecycle tracking, pre-delivery inspection (PDI) workflows, multi-warehouse transfer reconciliation, and warranty service dispatching.

#### 🧾 5. Automated Financial Auditing & Receipt Intelligence
- **Intelligent Expense Parsing**: Engineered an automated receipt and expense extraction engine (`kwix`) capable of handling crumpled thermal paper, receipts, and invoices.
- **Features**: Extracts line items, merchant tax numbers, and dates while performing automated anomaly detection, currency conversion, and duplicate expense identification.

#### 🔗 6. Enterprise ERP Financial Middleware (Oracle NetSuite)
- **Mission-Critical Financial Bridge**: Built an enterprise integration gateway (`netspark`) bridging transactional banking feeds, billing events, and e-commerce systems with **Oracle NetSuite ERP**.
- **Reliability**: Guarantees zero financial data loss through transactional idempotency, automated dead-letter retry queues, and end-to-end reconciliation logging.

#### ✍️ 7. Digital Signature & Certified E-Meterai Automation
- **Legal Compliance Workflow**: Developed middleware (`virtuesign`) integrating workspace databases (Lark Base) with official digital signature providers (**Mekari Sign** & PERURI e-meterai).
- **Impact**: Fully automated contract generation, legal stamp dispatch, signatory routing, and secure storage of government-certified signed PDF documents.

#### 👥 8. Edge AI Facial Recognition Kiosk & Smart Payroll Sync
- **On-Device Kiosk Attendance**: Deployed an edge computer vision kiosk for retail branch employees featuring anti-spoofing liveness detection and rapid facial recognition.
- **Integration**: Feeds verified clock-in/out timestamps in real time into payroll and attendance scheduling engines with zero manual timesheet audits.

#### ☸️ 9. Distributed Multi-Node K3s Cluster & Autonomous In-Cluster CI/CD
- **Cloud-Native Topology**: Orchestrated a 3-node distributed Kubernetes (K3s v1.36) cluster spanning cloud VPS nodes inter-connected via **Tailscale Mesh VPN (`100.x.x.x`)**.
- **Runner Fleet & Registry**: Deployed org-scoped in-cluster GitHub Actions self-hosted runners (`build-system` namespace) paired with an internal container registry. Completely bypassed third-party hosted runner minute limits and sped up Docker build rollouts by 4x.
- **Observability**: Implemented lightweight log aggregation using **VictoriaLogs + Vector/Fluentbit** (<100MB RAM footprint), replacing bulky ELK stacks.

#### 🤖 10. Multi-Agent AI Task Manager (`pm-orchestrator`) & Custom MCP Ecosystem
- **AI Agent Coordination**: Created `pm-orchestrator`, a persistent VPS background orchestrator coordinating multiple LLM agents (Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Google Antigravity) inside dedicated `tmux` sessions across 50+ repositories.
- **Custom MCP Servers**: Standardized Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers enabling LLM agents to safely interact with Lark Suite OpenAPIs, Cloudflare edge DNS, and backend frameworks.
- **DevEx Persistence**: Built remote headless IDE terminal hooks with automatic session resurrection, preserving live conversational context across network reboots.

#### 🛒 11. Regional Unified Commerce & B2B Supply Chain
- **Regional Marketplace Platform**: Architected a multi-tier e-commerce engine connecting regional MSMEs (UMKM), hotel hospitality chains, and food producers in Eastern Indonesia (Flores Timur).
- **Features**: Multi-vendor catalogue, automated inventory sync, payment gateway integration, and localized logistics dispatch.

#### ⚖️ 12. Conversational Legal & Tax AI Advisory
- **Domain-Specific AI Chatbots**: Engineered conversational legal and tax compliance assistants grounded with Indonesian legal corpus and tax calculation logic for corporate advisory.
